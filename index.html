FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need
to know before we start.

Quick answers to the most common questions about working together.

01 What are your rates? +

Rates are on request and depend on the project scope, deliverables, and usage. Every collaboration is quoted individually based on what you need.

02 How long does delivery take? +

Most projects are delivered within 10 to 12 business days after receiving the product and the brief. Timelines may vary depending on the complexity and number of videos.

03 How many revisions are included? +

Revisions are unlimited. I'll keep refining the content until it matches the brief and you're fully satisfied with the final result.

04 What rights do I get over the video? +

You get full usage rights over the final video. You can use it for organic social, paid ads, whitelisting, or any other purpose — no restrictions.

05 Do you accept physical products? +

Yes. I accept physical products shipped to me for filming. This is the best way to create authentic, hands-on content that truly showcases your product.

06 What product categories do you work with? +

I specialize in technology, gadgets, and home decor. If your product fits one of these categories, we're a great match. For other categories, feel free to reach out and I'll let you know if it's a fit.

07 What languages do you create content in? +

I create content in both English and Spanish — including voiceover, on-camera delivery, and captions. I can produce bilingual versions of the same video if needed.

08 How does the collaboration process work? +

The process is simple:

  1. You reach out with your product and goals.
  2. We align on the brief, style, deliverables, and timeline.
  3. I create the content and send a first draft.
  4. You request any changes you'd like.
  5. I deliver the final files once you're happy.
09 How do payments work? +

Payments are handled through PayPal. A deposit is required before production begins, and the remaining balance is due on delivery.
